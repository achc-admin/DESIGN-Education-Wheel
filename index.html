Before the Survey

ACHC is committed to helping you achieve accreditation. It starts with access to expertise. Your customer care specialist will point you to resources, including those of ACHCU, our educational division that offers an extensive collection of resources designed for staff education and development, and survey preparation. You also gain access to ongoing clinical regulatory support coordinated by your dedicated account advisor.

During the Survey

Our streamlined survey process is designed to help you gain value from your accreditation—by enhancing your patient care and your business operations. During your formal review, our industry-expert Surveyors will follow a comprehensive process that includes direct observations, interviews, and document review. While assessing compliance, they will answer questions and share insights into improvement opportunities.

Beyond the Survey

ACHC Surveyors will conduct the regular review of your organization, but ACHC shares communication throughout the accreditation cycle to help you maintain excellence and a culture of continuous improvement. Over time, your organization will maximize its potential for excellence supported by the quality improvement framework that ACHC Accreditation provides.
